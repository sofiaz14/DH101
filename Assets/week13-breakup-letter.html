Letter Artifact

Dear Dylan,

I've been trying to write this for weeks. It has taken me so long to write this because my Helix keeps softening it, calling it "too mean" and "counterproductive." I told it not to touch my letter and just let me write it because I was getting more and more confused each time. But this is my attempt at saying...

I am breaking up with you.

Here's what I can't stop thinking about... the weekend in California. You remembered I hated cilantro in my food, and I was so moved that you'd noticed. Then I found it in your Helix feed. "Sofia: STRONG hate for cilantro." You didn't even remember on your own; Helix had to remember for you. And I genuinely don't know if that's the same thing anymore. If it were 20 years ago and you wrote it in your iPhone notes to remember, it's completely different. But now, your Helix remembering and reminding you is not the same.

You're kind, Dylan. You're present... kinda. You ask good questions. But I've started to wonder how many of those questions Helix wrote for you just because it listens more than you, ..."she seems distant lately, ask about her mother Dylan"... and I can't find the difference between you and her anymore. Maybe there isn't one. Maybe that's the point. Maybe I'm being insane and romantic in some exhausting 20th century way about what "you" even means. But here's the other thing, I use my Helix too. It helped me figure out I was unhappy six months before I consciously knew it. It's been nudging me toward this letter since February. So who's breaking up with whom, exactly? Are you losing me, or is your AI losing mine?

I'd say let's talk in person, but I know Helix would prep us both with questions and ways to navigate the conversation, and we'd show up with talking points. I'd say call me, but it would transcribe it and send us each a summary with suggested follow ups. I don't know how to have a conversation with you that isn't mediated anymore. I'm not sure I remember what that felt like. For what it's worth, the parts of you I fell in love with, I think, were human. I hope they were. I'm going to keep believing that, because the alternative is scary to think about me falling for your Helix and not Dylan.

Enjoy your life Dylan... or should I say Helix.

Best wishes,
Sofia
