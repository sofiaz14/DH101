# Week 7 Reflection

Reflect 7: How does AI reshape global geographies of power, labor, and environment?

## My Response

AI is transforming global patterns of power, labor, and environmental impact by reorganizing the location of digital infrastructure, workers, and energy resources. As my map shows, AI systems rely on large-scale physical infrastructure, such as data centers and global networks of digital labor. These infrastructures are concentrated in regions with cheap land and reliable electricity, giving certain corporations and locations greater technological and economic power. At the same time, AI relies on human labor for tasks like data labeling and content moderation, which are often outsourced to regions with lower wages, creating a global division of digital labor that is essential yet invisible. AI infrastructure also has environmental consequences because data centers require enormous amounts of electricity and water, often linking technology development to energy production in specific regions.
