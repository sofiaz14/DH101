# Visibility in AI Networks

Visibility in AI Networks: Who is visible/invisible in AI networks? How does visualization reveal or obscure power?

## My Response

In AI networks, large technology companies tend to be the most visible because they are positioned at the center of innovation and the main technological decision making. On the other hand, content moderators and the communities whose data is used to train AI systems are often invisible, even though their labor and information are essential to how AI functions. Visualization uncovers power by showing which entities have the most influence, but it can also obscure power by hiding the labor and inequalities behind those connections. This shows that AI networks mirror existing power structures within the world instead of changing them.
