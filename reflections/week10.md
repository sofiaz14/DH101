# Week 10 Reflection

Reflect 10: How does AI change our relationship to play, rules, and narrative?

## My Response

AI changes our relationship to play, rules, and narrative by making decisions based on algorithms without any human judgment. This means players must learn how to navigate and "play" the system instead of being true to who they are. This changes the narrative because the story is no longer just about what happens, but about how the system evaluates and responds to your choices.
