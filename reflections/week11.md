# AI Invisible Work

AI Invisible Work: Who does the invisible work of AI, and who profits from it?

## My Response

The invisible work of AI and social media is done by content moderators. Content moderators are thousands of low paid contract workers, many located in the Global South, who spend their days reviewing disturbing content so that platforms stay clean for everyday users. These workers are deliberately kept hidden through NDAs and outsourcing chains, allowing tech companies like Meta to distance themselves from responsibility. Meanwhile, the platforms they protect generate billions in profit. For example, Facebook earned $6.9 billion in a single quarter, while moderators in the US earned just $28,800 a year, and those in Kenya earned as little as $429 a month. The people doing the most psychologically damaging work receive the least pay, the least protection, and the least recognition, while executives and shareholders collect the rewards. The system is designed to extract value from marginalized workers while keeping their labor invisible to the consumers who benefit from it every day.
