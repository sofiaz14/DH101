# AI & Endless Creativity

AI & Endless Creativity: What is creativity when AI can generate endlessly? Where is the human in generative work?

## My Response

With the ability to produce infinite AI creations, creativity becomes more about the skill of deciding than the act of producing. As humans, we dictate what to ask for, what to change, and what to keep. The human presence in generative work lives in the prompt and the curation of every choice to refine or reject an output. This is a creative act in itself. Building my own generator, I quickly realized that I was not controlling the exact image that appeared, but I was designing the conditions that made certain images possible. That distinction felt important because I was the architect of the system and the editor of its results, which is a form of authorship in itself. In that way, the loss of pixel level control did not feel like a loss of creativity, just a change in how that creativity was used.
