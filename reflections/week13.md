# Week 13 Reflection

Reflect 13: Do we imagine AI futures as utopian, dystopian, or something in between? What do those visions reveal about us?

## My Response

I imagine the future of AI as in-between. I think it will be a real yet other space that exists alongside our own, distorting contemporary society like a mirror. I imagine the AI future will be layered, liberating some areas while displacing others. Our vision of AI reveals deep anxieties about control and inequality that already define our present lives. We fear AI dominance not because machines are inherently threatening, but because we live in systems where power is already unevenly distributed. Ultimately, what we imagine for AI is a projection of who we already are.
