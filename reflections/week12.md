# Environmental Impact Report

Environmental Impact Report: Is AI sustainable? What ecological trade-offs are we willing to accept?

## My Response

AI in its current form is not fully sustainable. The energy, water, and materials required to build and run these systems at scale place real burdens on ecosystems that are already under stress. The trade offs in my opinion are far too harmful. In exchange for all the damage we get tools that primarily benefit wealthy individuals and corporations while the environmental costs fall disproportionately on poorer communities and ecosystems. What makes this difficult is that those trade offs are rarely presented as a choice, because most people using AI have no idea what it costs the planet. Sustainability is possible in theory, particularly if the industry transitions to renewable energy sources and is held to stricter standards, but that requires systemic regulation and not individual behavior change.
